01 — Why we exist

Not quite a newspaper. Not quite a rebellion.

The Absurd Ordinary is an independent literary and cultural publication interested in the things we overlook, the questions we avoid, the stories we carry and the wonderfully ridiculous business of being human.

02 — Our philosophy

Take words seriously. Take ourselves lightly.

We believe literature belongs everywhere. In conversations, in uncomfortable questions, in everyday observations and in the stories that rarely find a place to be heard.

We make room for humour, reflection, culture, criticism, imagination and the occasional unnecessary investigation.

03 — Beyond the page

Words should occasionally leave the paper.

Through The Ordinary Matters, we hope to connect storytelling with awareness, social understanding and meaningful action. Because some things deserve more than being read and forgotten.

04 — The person behind it

An unreasonable curiosity about everything.

Founded by Saqeeb Aroofa, a writer who believes that ordinary life is rarely as ordinary as it pretends to be.
