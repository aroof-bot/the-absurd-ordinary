Our mission

Literature first. Humanity always.

The Ordinary Matters is the social-impact initiative of The Absurd Ordinary. It connects storytelling, awareness and practical action, so that some of what we write can turn into something that helps.

We don’t promise to solve every problem. We aim to make a meaningful contribution within our capacity, and to tell you honestly what we did and what happened.

How it works

Four steps, in this order.

1
Words

We publish a story or article that gives an overlooked experience a voice.

2
Awareness

We share research, interviews and experiences that help readers understand the issue.

3
Action

Where we can, we work with a verified organisation on something practical, such as a book collection or reading initiative.

4
Accountability

We publish an honest report on what was collected, where it went and what was achieved.

Long-term areas of interest

Five programmes.

  • 01Pages for EveryoneLiteracy, access to books, reading programmes and educational opportunities.
  • 02Voices UnheardStories of people and communities that deserve greater representation.
  • 03The Conversation ProjectResponsible conversations about mental health, stigma, loneliness, social pressure and emotional wellbeing.
  • 04Roots & WordsLanguage, regional literature, cultural memory, translations and overlooked literary traditions.
  • 05Small Acts, Real ChangePractical community action around education, accessibility, environmental responsibility and social inequality.

Our promises

The rules we hold ourselves to.

  1. Informed consent before publishing personal stories, photographs or interviews.
  2. Identities protected wherever disclosure could create risk.
  3. Social and political claims fact-checked.
  4. Reporting, opinion and creative writing clearly distinguished.
  5. No sensationalising poverty, trauma or suffering.
  6. No promising help or outcomes we can’t deliver.
  7. Organisations verified before we recommend or partner with them.
  8. Donations and expenses kept transparent.
  9. No collecting sensitive information unnecessarily.
  10. Factual mistakes corrected openly.
  11. Meaningful action over performative activism.

What we are: an independent publication and community initiative. We are not a registered NGO or charity.
